Turing Arena
In this app, several players are placed in a chatroom with two AI models, but they aren't told which players are human and which ones are AI. Each player takes turns sending messages, and after 10 messages are sent each player casts their votes as to who was human and who was AI. The AI players do not get a vote. Then the results are displayed on "results.html"

161 def _human_delay(self, latest_message: str, reply: str):
162 """Sleep to simulate thinking/typing time based on message length."""
163 latest_len = len(latest_message or "")
164 reply_len = len(reply or "")
165 think_time = 0.6 + (latest_len / 80.0)
166 type_speed = random.uniform(12, 20) # characters per second
167 type_time = reply_len / type_speed
168 jitter = random.uniform(0.5, 1.4)
169 delay = (think_time + type_time) * jitter
170 delay = max(0.8, min(delay, 6.0))
171 time.sleep(delay)
