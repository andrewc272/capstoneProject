Turing Arena
In this app, several players are placed in a chatroom with two AI models, but they aren't told which players are human and which ones are AI. Each player takes turns sending messages, and after 10 messages are sent each player casts their votes as to who was human and who was AI. The AI players do not get a vote. Then the results are displayed on "results.html"
